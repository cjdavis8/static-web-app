Sandor Clegane

Westerman knight of questionable morals.

Allegiance: Joffrey

About the Character

Sandor Clegane is a member of House Clegane and is the younger brother of Ser Gregor Clegane. Sandor is nicknamed the Hound for his fierce nature and unquestioning obedience to House Lannister and for the three dogs featured in his family's arms. As a child, Sandor received gruesome facial burns when Gregor shoved his face into a brazier.[6] He has come to loathe fire, his brother, and the hypocrisy of knighthood in general. The sworn shield of Prince Joffrey Baratheon, Sandor is considered one of the most dangerous fighters in Westeros.
